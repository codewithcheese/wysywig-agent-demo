The fiscal year 2024 marked a significant milestone for ACME Corporation. Our strategic initiatives have resulted in unprecedented growth across all business units.

3. Strategic Outlook

Looking ahead to 2025, we have identified several strategic priorities that will drive our continued growth and market leadership.

3.1 Market Expansion

We plan to enter five new regional markets while strengthening our presence in existing territories. This expansion will be supported by increased sales team capacity and localized marketing initiatives.

3.2 Product Innovation

Our R&D investments will focus on next-generation solutions that address emerging customer needs. We anticipate launching three new product lines by mid-2025.

3.3 Operational Excellence

Continued optimization of our operations will remain a priority. We target a 15% improvement in operational efficiency through process automation and lean practices.
